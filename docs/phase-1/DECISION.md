## 1. Chosen angle

**Robustness to LLM-generated misinformation, with calibrated selective prediction.**

We will build an open-weight RAG fact-checker (claim → retrieve → verify → rationale) and study, in a controlled experiment, how its accuracy and calibration degrade under three families of LLM-generated style attack — and how an *insufficient-evidence abstention* mechanism can recover trustworthy behaviour by trading coverage for risk.

The system is the **Innovation** half (Dockerised, deployed locally with a typed FastAPI surface for the dashboard arriving in Phase 10). The controlled experiment is the **Research** half. They share infrastructure: the same evaluation harness, the same baseline, the same datasets.

## 2. Why it's open

Three independent lines of work in 2024–2025 converge on this gap:

- **LLM-written misinformation is systematically harder to detect** than human-written misinformation with the same propositional content. SheepDog (P21, KDD 2024) measured a 38% F1 drop for SOTA detectors under LLM style attacks. Chen (P22, ICLR 2024) and the *Nature Communications* 2025 detection-limits paper (P23) reproduced this finding across detectors and paraphrasers. P21's defence (style-agnostic training) recovers part of the drop but is reported on a single attack family.
- **Confident-wrong verdicts harm users more than no verdict at all.** The PNAS study (P05) and Quelle & Bovet's Frontiers paper (P04) both find that LLM fact-checking *reduces* user accuracy on true headlines when the model is uncertain but presents itself as confident. Calibrated abstention is therefore a deployment requirement, not a nicety.
- **No public work jointly evaluates these two failure modes.** The closest piece is faithfulness-aware uncertainty quantification for RAG (P24, 2025), which targets rationale faithfulness rather than detector-vs-paraphraser robustness. The intersection — *how does abstention quality change under adversarial paraphrase?* — is, to our reading, untouched.

The angle also pairs cleanly with the course PDF's "fakes generator / Virtual Hacker" suggestion: our paraphraser is exactly that, and a sister team (or a second component of our team) can deepen it independently.

## 3. Hypothesis

> **H1.** A RAG fact-checker calibrated with insufficient-evidence abstention preserves ≥ 80% of its in-distribution accuracy at coverage ≥ 0.7 when evaluated against three families of LLM-generated paraphrase attacks, whereas the same system without abstention loses ≥ 15 absolute F1 points on at least one attack family.

H1 is falsifiable: we report (i) full-coverage F1 deltas under attack, (ii) risk–coverage curves with bootstrap confidence intervals, (iii) the abstain ECE (P32) on attacked vs clean inputs. The numerical thresholds (15-pt drop, 0.7 coverage, 80% retention) are calibrated against the SheepDog drop magnitudes (P21) and the SelectLLM-typical 5–15 point recovery range (P32).

## 4. Proposed method (sketch)

1. **Baseline detector.** Open-weight RAG pipeline along the AVeriTeC-2 winning shape (P07–P09): claim decomposition → BM25 + dense retrieval → 7–8B open-weight verifier (Llama-3.1-8B or Qwen-2.5-7B) producing structured `{verdict, rationale, evidence_ids}`.
2. **Adversarial paraphraser.** A second open-weight LLM rewriting fake claims in three styles (newswire, tabloid, social-thread) while preserving propositional content. Acts as the course's "Virtual Hacker" companion.
3. **Abstention head.** An insufficient-evidence detector that fuses (a) verifier softmax / token-level uncertainty, (b) retrieval-quality signal (top-k similarity, evidence-claim NLI alignment), (c) post-hoc temperature scaling. Output is a confidence ∈ [0, 1] and an abstention threshold τ.
4. **Risk–coverage evaluation harness.** AURC, abstain-ECE (per P32), accuracy-at-coverage, slice metrics by attack family.

Architectural details are deliberately deferred to Phase 4 (ADRs).

## 5. Datasets and metrics

| Use | Dataset | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Primary in-distribution eval | **AVeriTeC v2** (FEVER-2025 task) | Real-world claims with web evidence; the current SOTA benchmark (P07). Time-ordered split. |
| Secondary in-distribution | FakeNewsNet | For drift-slice and cross-domain robustness only. Not headline-reportable. |
| Adversarial slices | **LLM-paraphrase attack set** | Generated by us with Llama-3.1-8B / Qwen-2.5-7B; three styles. Released with the repo. |
| Optional multilingual | MuMiN small | Stretch goal for Phase 7. |

**Metrics:** F1 / macro-F1 (verdict), ECE + MCE (calibration), AURC and accuracy@coverage (selective prediction), AVeriTeC score (Ev2R recall) for benchmark comparability, per-attack-family slice metrics, retrieval-error share.

## 6. Rejected alternatives

| Angle | Why rejected (one paragraph each) |
|---|---|
| **Angle 2 — retrieval-failure abstention only** | Strong on its own (P20, P24) but the failure-mode envelope is narrower; without the LLM-paraphrase axis the project loses the "machine-vs-machine arms race" framing the course PDF leans toward. We retain the retrieval-quality signal as one input to the abstention head — so the work is absorbed, not lost. |
| **Angle 3 — faithful rationales** | Genuinely open (P25 Drift, P26 HalluTree) but the evaluation is the bottleneck: faithfulness metrics are unsettled and a small team cannot run a credible human study in eight weeks. Risk to the RES half too high. |
| **Angle 5 — temporal drift / continual learning** | Real and important (P30, P31), but a longitudinal split on AVeriTeC requires careful date metadata curation we'd lose 1–2 weeks to. Out of scope. |
| **Angle 6 — multimodal misinformation** | MMM-Fact (P20) and the agentic multimodal paper (P36) are exciting, but image+text plumbing roughly doubles the engineering load. We will accept multimodal as a "next-milestone" candidate after the text-only system is shipped. |
| **Angle 7 — cross-lingual transfer** | High value (CheckThat! 2025 covers 20+ languages) but compute-heavy at scale. Defer to a follow-on milestone. |
| **End-to-end LLM zero-shot detector** | Repeatedly shown to harm users when miscalibrated (P04, P05). Including it as a baseline only, never as the system. |

## 7. Risk register (top 3)

1. **Compute access for the 7–8B verifier.** Mitigation: 4-bit quantisation + Colab Pro fallback; if persistent, downgrade verifier to a 3B class model (Phi-3 / Qwen-2.5-3B) for the full study and use the 7B model only for spot-check ablations.
2. **Adversarial-paraphrase quality confound.** If paraphrases drift from the original propositional content, the F1 drop is no longer attributable to "style attack". Mitigation: human spot-check on 10% of the attack set; reject paraphrases that fail an NLI-entailment check against the original.
3. **Scope creep onto rationale faithfulness.** Easy to lose two weeks on the rationale verbatim faithfulness rabbit hole. Mitigation: rationales are *output*, not *evaluated* — they ship in the artefact and the demo, but are not a metric in the controlled experiment unless we get a clean week-7 surplus.
